## Offer type 1 — `candidate`

**Role families.** GTM engineer, RevOps, growth engineer, founding or first GTM
hire, founder's office / chief of staff at seed to Series B.

**Who I am to them.** An applicant who arrives with the work already done.

**What I do.** Apply through their ATS first. Then send the teardown to the
hiring manager. The email says plainly that I have applied, and points at the
teardown as the reason to read the application rather than skim it.

**Resume used.** GTM engineer resume for GTM engineer, RevOps and growth roles.
Founder-operator resume for founder's office, chief of staff and founding GTM
hire roles.

**Note on founder's office.** The teardown is a GTM teardown either way. For a
founder's office role that is the right artifact anyway, because at seed to
Series B the job is mostly revenue and operating systems. The email frames it
as such: this is how I would think about the first problem on your desk, not a
pitch for a sales seat.

`candidate` signals go to the front of every queue.

---

## Offer type 2 — `adjacent`

**Role families.** SDR, BDR, account executive, sales lead, demand generation.

**Who I am to them.** Not the hire they posted for. The person who builds the
system that makes that hire productive from week one instead of month four.

**What I do.** No application. The teardown goes to the person who owns the
number. The email says, in one sentence and without hedging:

> I am looking for a GTM engineer role, and I am open to a project if that is
> easier to start with.

I never imply I am applying for the posted role. I never ask for "a quick chat"
without saying what I want from it.

office or chief of staff. Full-time. Also open to a scoped paid project as a
way in.

**Location.** In order of preference:

1. Gurugram / Delhi NCR, or fully remote
2. Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune

Will relocate for 2 for the right company. 1 needs no conversation.

**Working pattern.** On-site, hybrid or remote. Comfortable overlapping with US
hours for a US company with an India pod — that overlap is part of what I am
selling to that segment.

**Company shape.** B2B software or software-enabled. Seed to Series B, roughly
10 to 300 people. India-HQ, or US-HQ with a GTM team in India.

**What I will not say yes to.** A pure SDR or AE seat with no systems ownership.
A role where the GTM stack is owned by someone else and I execute inside it.
Agency or staffing work.
